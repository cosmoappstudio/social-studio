Order of sources: Simple Icons (CC0 SVG, https://cdn.simpleicons.org/<slug>), official brand PNGs (e.g. gstatic product icons for Google Ads/Analytics/Tag Manager/AdMob/Play Console: https://www.gstatic.com/images/branding/product/2x/<name>_96dp.png), site apple-touch-icon, Google s2 favicons as last resort. Prefer official multicolour over mono. Save as `assets/logo_<slug>.(svg|png)`, preview on a contact sheet, list unresolved ones. Ask before downloading? State filename+source in the report. Logos are used descriptively (integrations), not as endorsements.
